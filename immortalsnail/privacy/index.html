Privacy Policy — The Immortal Snail

Effective 12 June 2026 · The Immortal Snail: It Follows (com.lareon.immortalsnail) · Developer: Lareon

The short version. The Immortal Snail runs entirely on your device. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and does not request internet access — the app is incapable of sending your data anywhere. Your location is read on your device to move the snail, and it never leaves your device.

Location

The game's core mechanic is a snail that slowly pursues your real position. To do this, the app reads your device's precise location while you are using the app.

  • Your location is processed entirely on your device. It is used to update the game state — the snail's pursuit, distances, events — and nothing else.
  • Your location is never transmitted. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send your location — or anything else — off your device.
  • The app does not collect location in the background. When the app is closed, nothing is tracking you. (The snail, regrettably, persists.)
  • You can revoke location permission at any time in Android settings. The game falls back to a fixed default position and becomes correspondingly less personal — and less frightening.

What is stored on your device

The app saves your game locally using Android's app storage: your save (snail position, name, run history), inventory and salt, statistics and trophies, postcards, and settings such as the notification toggle.

  • This data stays in the app's private storage on your device.
  • It is deleted in full when you uninstall the app.
  • You can also erase your save from within the app at any time.

What we do not do

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no way to identify you.
  • No analytics or tracking. No usage data, crash reporting, device identifiers, or fingerprinting.
  • No advertising. The app contains no ad SDKs.
  • No third-party data sharing. There is no data to share and no network connection to share it over.
  • No internet access. The released app does not declare the INTERNET permission. You can verify this yourself by inspecting the APK's permissions.

Notifications

The app can show local notifications — for example, warnings as the snail draws near. These are scheduled on your device by the app itself; they do not come from a server. Notifications are optional: you can turn them off inside the app or in Android settings. The app also asks Android to restore its scheduled notifications after your device reboots; this involves no data beyond the locally stored schedule.

Children

The Immortal Snail is not directed at children under 13. Its themes of slow, inevitable pursuit are intended for a general audience. In any case, the app collects no personal data from any user of any age.

Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR and similar laws)

Because the app collects no personal data and transmits nothing, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, export, or delete. The data on your device is under your direct control: revoke permissions in Android settings, erase your save in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling.

Changes to this policy

Future versions of the app may add optional features that require the internet — for example advertising or in-app purchases. If that happens, this policy will be updated before such a version is released, the effective date above will change, and the app's store listing will reflect the new data practices. The current policy describes the app as it ships today, and today it sends nothing.
